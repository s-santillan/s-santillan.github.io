## Project Description

**Maria Health** is an online platform providing access to healthcare for all Filipinos. This is achieved through a marketplace for prepaid health coverage as well as a matching process where small-to-medium enterprise owners are presented eligible plans from top HMOs in the Philippines based on factors such as company size, industry, and the like. This application of actuarial logic reduces the time and effort needed to present users with options side-by-side, assisting them in choosing the plan that best fits their needs.

| My role | Developer (75% Frontend, 25% Backend) |
| --- | --- |
| **Core team size** | less than 20 |
| **Responsibilities** | <ul><li>Manage "copy" Django app (microservices API)</li><li>Collaborate with designers on content design (there was no content designer)</li><li>Implement site frontend</li><li>Onboard new team members</li><li>Train sales & product team to use the backend dashboard</li><li>Write Selenium tests</li></ul> |
| **Documentation produced** | <ul><li>Onboarding decks for sales & product</li><li>Code documentation (specifically for payment partners & glue logic)</li></ul> |
| **Frameworks** | Angular, Vue, Django |
| **My tools** | Trello, Pivotal Tracker, GVim,VSCode, Figma, BrowserStack |

- I coded frontend pages in Angular for a year, following design specs. These included:
  - Contact Us
  - Cart
  - Various landing pages attached to campaigns
  - Plan Comparison pages.
- I handled the migration to Vue and Webpack in 2017, rewriting the entire site's frontend into Vue.
- I created a **microcopy app** in the backend, with shortcodes to every line of text in the web site. Coupled with **VWO Insights A/B testing**, this allowed the product team to update old copy to a better-converting text anywhere on the site, instantly from the dashboard, and without the need to go through developers. This resulted in faster conversion improvements.
- I wrote documentation on how to use the admin dashboard for use by Sales and Product.
- I wrote Selenium tests (using the Python webdriver) for automated Cart testing.
