# Selected Projects, 2020-2026

<OpenerText>
Developer marketing was in full swing from about 2017 to 2023. During this time, Vultr, DO and many more were pumping the internet full of content for developers of all stripes. They needed writers who wrote engaging **end-to-end code projects** and code samples, and I found the work agreed with me.

Alongside writing about devtools, colleagues occasionally tapped me to help out with product development in games.
</OpenerText>

An online racing game. Its scope changed in response to the media trends of the late 2010s and early 2020s, notably the rise of Draft Kings, Fan Duel and the like. I utilized my background as a web developer and game designer in this role, which made me a **unique fit to design the UX** and communicate with the developers in their language.

I designed and mapped the UX flow for the following pages:

- Landing page
- Login and Sign up
- Account management
- Racing UX
- Race results UX
- Racer stats

## DeepSource

DeepSource is a code analyzer. In the early 2020s their product marketing strategy aimed to attract developers through tutorials and guides specific to code analysis. I wrote product comparisons for static analysis tools (SpotBugs, for example) as well as articles on programming concepts and frontend frameworks.

For this type of writing, the **quality of the code examples is paramount**.
